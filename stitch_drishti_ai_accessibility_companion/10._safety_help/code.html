Safety & Help

shield

Always continue using your white cane, guide dog, or established mobility techniques. Drishti AI is a supplementary awareness tool.

check_circle What Drishti AI can do

  • Identify supported nearby obstacles using your phone camera and Cactus Mirage.
  • Deliver concise spoken alerts locally on your device, even without internet.

block What Drishti AI cannot do

  • It cannot guarantee safe road crossings or detect every hazard (such as drop-offs or potholes).
  • It does not provide turn-by-turn maps, traffic signal advice, or distance measurements.

Quick controls & help

Stop quickly: Tap the large crimson Stop Assistance button at the bottom of the Live Assistance screen.

Camera issues: Ensure camera permission is granted in Settings and no other app is using the camera.

No audio: Raise device media volume and verify Speech alerts are turned on in Settings.
